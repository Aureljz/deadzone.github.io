Crafting Guide

Mining System

Copper and iron are the main resources. You need a pickaxe to mine them.

Pickaxes can't be crafted. Find one by looting.
Copper Ore

Found in the quarry and the mountain mines. Used for reinforced logs, barbed wire and roped ladders.

Iron Ore

Found in the quarry and the mountain mines. Smelt it into iron ingots. Raw iron is also used for reinforced concrete.

Known extraction sites
The Quarry

A big open pit with copper and iron in the walls. Lots of ore, but you're out in the open.

⬤ Copper ⬤ Iron
Mountain Mines

Small abandoned mines in the mountains. Less ore than the quarry, and easy to miss.

⬤ Copper ⬤ Iron

Wood Chopping

Chopping trees gives you sticks. You need them for planks, ladders and most recipes.

Axes can't be crafted. Loot one or trade for it.
Stick Drop

60% chance to drop sticks, 40% chance of nothing. A drop gives 1 to 4 sticks.

Getting an Axe

Find one by looting or get it by trading. There are several types, they all work for chopping.

Forest Chopping

Any tree works.

60% drop chance 1–4 sticks requires axe
Axe Types

Hatchets, mauls and saws all work for chopping.

lootable tradeable not craftable

Raid System

Right now the sledgehammer is the only way to break into a base.

Sledgehammer

The only raiding tool at the moment. It can't be crafted, so loot it or trade for it. It's worth a lot.

not craftable loot or trade ⚠ high value
Loot

Search buildings, garages and warehouses.

Trade

Some survivors may have one. It will cost a lot.

More raiding tools may be added later.

Base Tiers

There are four tiers. Each one takes more hits to break than the one before.

The Four Tiers

Left to right, weakest to strongest.

Vertical Planks
Tier 1 — weakest

The basic one. Easy to craft, breaks fast.

Reinforced Log
Tier 2

Wood with a metal band. Takes more hits than plain planks.

Reinforced Concrete
Tier 3

Stronger than the two wood tiers.

Rounded Iron Bricks
Tier 4 — strongest

The strongest tier. Takes the most hits to break.

A sledgehammer can break every tier.

Keypad Door

Set a code for your door, keep it to yourself and don't forget it.

Keypad Door

Only opens with the code. A forgotten password can't be recovered, and you'll be locked out of your own base.

craftable code protected ⚠ don't forget your password
Can Be Raided

A sledgehammer can break it, but it uses up a lot of durability.

Stay Hidden

If nobody knows where your door is, nobody can raid it.
